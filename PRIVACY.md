# Privacy policy

FibeStation runs in your browser. Your music, images and settings stay on your computer: the app has no server of its own and no accounts, it uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and it loads nothing from other sites. Here is what that means in detail.

Last updated: 3 October 2026.

## Who is responsible

FibeStation is a private, non-commercial project by FionaPreroll ([github.com/FionaPreroll](https://github.com/FionaPreroll)). For anything about your data, write to fipreroll+app@gmail.com.

## What stays in your browser

The app keeps what it needs in your browser's storage for this site (local storage, IndexedDB and the origin private file system), on your computer:

- your settings and presets, and the background and logo images you add;
- for each track: its cues, markers, tempo, name, and the cover and colours you gave it, and its analysis (the waveform and the beat grid), found again by a fingerprint of the file's content;
- the queue, and in Chrome and Edge the access to its files, so that it survives a reload;
- exports in progress, so that they can go on after a crash; and in browsers that cannot write straight to a file, the finished videos until you download them.

None of it is sent anywhere. The app needs it for what you use, so it asks for no consent. It asks the browser to keep this storage, so that it is not cleared when the disk runs low. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes all of it. A backup (**Settings → Save a backup**) is a file on your computer, and goes only where you take it.

## Music, live input and devices

- **Music files** are read and analysed in your browser. They are never uploaded.
- **Live input** (a microphone, an audio interface, another app or a browser tab) is analysed while it plays. It is not recorded, and not sent anywhere.
- **A DJ controller** (Web MIDI) is used only when you connect it in the app.
- **Videos** are rendered on your computer and saved where you choose.

The app gets a microphone, the sound of another app, MIDI devices or a folder only when you choose them, and the browser asks you first.

## What goes over the network

The app is a set of files that your browser loads from the site. While it is open, it asks the site for the current version (`version.json`), every 30 minutes and when you come back to its tab, to offer an update; **Licences** loads the list of licences when you open it. That is all: the app connects to no other server, and its content security policy does not let it. When you follow a link out of the app, it sends no referrer.

## Hosting: Cloudflare

The site, [fibestation.fipreroll.workers.dev](https://fibestation.fipreroll.workers.dev), is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (Cloudflare Workers). To deliver the app and to protect the site from attacks, Cloudflare processes what every request carries: your IP address, the time, the address asked for, and what your browser says about itself (its user agent). Cloudflare may process it in the USA too; it is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. The developer keeps no logs of visits and sees only totals, such as the number of requests.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in delivering the app reliably and securely. More in [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Bug reports and ideas

- **By mail:** **Report a bug** in the help opens a mail to fipreroll+app@gmail.com in your mail program. It holds what you write, and what the app adds: the version, your browser (its user agent) and the address of the page, or the details of an error. You see all of it before you send it, and can change it. Your address and your mail are used to answer and to fix the problem, and deleted when they are no longer needed for that. The address is at Gmail, so Google receives the mail ([Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)).
- **The system check** in the help is shown only to you. **Copy the report** puts it on your clipboard, to paste where you like. It names your browser, system, graphics chip, number of threads, memory, encoders and storage.
- **On GitHub:** issues are public, and [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) applies. Leave out what you would not want to show publicly.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in answering your report and improving the app.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to know what personal data about you is processed (Art. 15), to have it corrected (Art. 16) or deleted (Art. 17), to restrict its processing (Art. 18), to get it in a common format (Art. 20), and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). Write to fipreroll+app@gmail.com. You may also complain to a data protection authority (Art. 77). There is no automated decision-making or profiling.

What the app keeps in your browser, only you can see and delete: in your browser's settings for the site.

When the app changes what it does with data, this page changes with it, and **What's new** says so.
